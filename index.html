The SVG always exports in millimeters, so imperial entries don't pick up rounding. Every cut path carries shaper:cutDepth set to thickness plus overcut. Slots and holes are inside cuts (black stroke, white fill); the outline is an outside cut (black fill). The anchor is a red, strokeless right triangle whose right-angle corner is the anchor point, short leg on X, long leg on Y.
